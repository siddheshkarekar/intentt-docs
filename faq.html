Getting started

What is Intentt?

Intentt is an all-in-one habit tracker, task manager and focus timer. It's built around a simple idea: small intentions, repeated daily, become who you are.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud. Open the app and start — everything is stored on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Intentt never needs an internet connection to work. (The only time it goes online is for a Google Play purchase or a Play Store rating, both handled by Google.)

Habits & streaks

What habit schedules can I create?

Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or fully custom — for example "3 times a week", "2 times a month", or "every 10 days". Pick whatever matches how you actually live.

What is a "measurable" habit?

A habit tracked with a number instead of a simple tick — 20 minutes of meditation, 8 glasses of water, 30 pages read. Set a target and choose whether you want to hit at least that number (e.g. minutes read) or at most (e.g. cigarettes). Your daily values show up in the grid and in your insights.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts consecutive completions of a habit. Your current streak is your run right now; your longest streak is your best ever. Miss a scheduled day and the current streak resets — unless you're in Holiday Mode.

What is Holiday Mode?

Going away? In Settings → Holiday Mode (a Pro feature) you set a date range. During those dates your habit streaks are frozen — a missed day won't reset your progress — and habit reminders are paused. Your streak picks up right where it left off when you're back. Task reminders keep firing.

What do the symbols in the habit grid mean?

Each habit row shows one cell per day. Here's the whole language:

SymbolMeaning
✓ Done — you completed the habit that day. This is what builds your streak.
✗ Missed — you marked the day as not done. A miss breaks the current streak (unless the day falls inside Holiday Mode).
? Due — the habit was scheduled for that day and hasn't been logged yet. It's a nudge, not a failure. This marker only appears if you switch on Settings → Show due-day markers.
– Nothing logged — either the habit wasn't scheduled that day, or the day is simply blank.
34 A number — for measurable habits, the value you logged that day (34 minutes, 8 glasses, and so on) instead of a tick.

Tap any day to change it. Tapping cycles through: nothing → Done → Missed → nothing. For measurable habits, tapping lets you type the value.

Can I edit a past day?

Yes. Open the habit's grid (or its Insights) and tap any day to change it. Forgot to log yesterday? Just tap it.

Tasks

What can a task have?

A priority (High / Medium / Low), a category, a due date, a reminder, notes, and subtasks to break bigger work into steps.

What are subtasks?

Subtasks break a bigger task into steps, so "Plan the trip" becomes a checklist instead of one intimidating line.

  • Adding them: add subtasks while creating a task, or tap any task to open it and add them later.
  • Tracking progress: the task row shows a checklist chip like 1/3 — how many subtasks are done out of the total — so you can see progress without opening the task.
  • Ticking off: open the task and check each subtask as you finish it.
  • A helpful guard: if you complete a task that still has open subtasks, Intentt asks whether they're really all done — and offers to take you back to review them, so nothing gets closed by accident.

Can I turn something from another app into a task?

Yes. Select text anywhere on your phone, tap Share, and choose Intentt — it becomes a pre-filled new task.

What's the calendar strip at the top?

It shows your week at a glance, with a dot on days that have something due. Tap any date to see just that day's tasks and habits.

Focus timer

How does the focus timer work?

It's a Pomodoro-style timer: a focus block, then a break, repeating. Choose Classic (25/5), Short (15/3), or set your own Custom lengths.

What is Focus Mode?

A toggle that silences notifications while you focus (priority calls still come through), and restores your normal settings the moment you stop. It needs Do Not Disturb access, which Android will ask you to grant once.

What are focus sounds?

Optional ambient audio — Rain, Wind, Static or Deep — that loops while you focus, to mask distractions. It plays only during focus blocks and pauses on breaks.

Can I link a session to a task or habit?

Yes — start a focus session from a task or habit (the ▶ button) and the time is attributed to it, so your insights show where your time actually went.

Reminders & notifications

My reminders aren't showing up — how do I fix it?

This is almost always your phone's battery saver stopping the app in the background, not a bug in Intentt. Android manufacturers are aggressive about this. Fix it once and it stays fixed:

1. Allow notifications — Settings → Apps → Intentt → Notifications → allow.

2. Turn off battery optimisation — Settings → Apps → Intentt → Battery → choose Unrestricted / Don't optimise.

3. Enable Auto-start (this is the big one on Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO, Realme, OPPO, vivo, OnePlus) — find "Auto-start", "Autolaunch" or "Startup manager" in your phone's settings or Security app and enable it for Intentt. Without this, your phone can block reminders after a reboot.

4. Lock the app in Recents — open Recent apps, and lock/pin Intentt so the system won't kill it.

Exact menu names vary by phone brand and Android version — search your Settings for "auto-start" or "battery" if you can't find them.

Why do reminders need "Alarms & reminders" permission?

So a reminder fires at the exact minute you set. Without exact alarms, Android may delay a reminder by many minutes, which defeats the point.

Can I make reminders stay until I act on them?

Yes — Settings → Notifications → Persistent reminders. They then won't disappear from swiping or "Clear all".

Intentt Pro

What do I get with Pro?

Full habit capacity, premium themes, advanced insights (trends, rankings, best days, focus breakdowns) and Holiday Mode.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Intentt Pro is a one-time purchase. Buy it once, keep it.

I bought Pro but it's not showing / I changed phones

Go to Settings → Premium → Restore purchase. As long as you're signed into the same Google account that made the purchase, Pro will come back. Make sure you're online.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds follow the Google Play refund policy.

Your data, backup & privacy

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Intentt collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup → Export writes a backup file wherever you choose (Drive, Downloads, etc.). On the new phone, install Intentt and use Restore and pick that file.

Restoring replaces all current tasks and habits in the app — so export first if you have data you want to keep.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items in the app, or uninstall Intentt to remove everything from your device. Since nothing is stored on any server, there's nothing left behind.
